# VEXISX

**The AI coworker built for Indian advocates.**

VEXISX researches, analyses and drafts alongside you, in your browser at [app.vexisx.in](https://app.vexisx.in). Add a matter's documents (FIR, chargesheet, orders) and VEXISX prepares the summary, key dates and evidence, finds relevant judgments on Indian Kanoon, and drafts the bail application, reply or notice you need, ready to export to Word or PDF.

---

## What it does

- **Draft** petitions, applications, replies and notices from your case documents or the facts you describe
- **Research** case law, with every judgment it relies on listed for you to verify
- **Organise** each matter: summary, timeline, evidence and drafts in one place
- **Ask** legal questions and get direct answers, with case law when you want it

## How we handle your data

- **Encrypted in transit**: everything you send travels over TLS
- **Zero data retention**: the AI provider keeps nothing once your result is produced, and nothing you send trains any model
- **Stored in your browser**: your cases, chats and calendar stay in your browser, encrypted, not on our servers
- Full details in our [Privacy Policy](https://vexisx.in/privacy-policy)
